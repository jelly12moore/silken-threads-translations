Chapter 11 — Stay Away From Me!

Cute Beast World: Beast Husband, Memeda!

There were quite a lot of planks, and it would take several trips to haul them all back.

"You take the first load," Lin HuanHuan said to Bai Di. "I'll stay here and watch the rest."

"The forest is dangerous," Bai Di said, reluctant. "I can't leave you here alone. Come with me — the wood isn't worth guarding. It doesn't matter if someone takes it."

"It does matter. You worked so hard to cut all of this. I'm not letting anyone steal it."

Her words left a warm feeling in Bai Di's chest. He looked at her helplessly. "I really don't know what to do with you."

He lifted her up and settled her on a tree branch, then tucked a bone and a small bag of sweet fruits into her arms.

"Stay right here and don't move. I'll be back before you know it."

"Okay!" Lin HuanHuan nodded.

Bai Di loaded up the planks and set off at a brisk pace.

Left alone in the tree, Lin HuanHuan had nothing to do. She pulled out the atlas, settled herself comfortably on the branch, and set about reading while snacking on sweet fruits.

She was on her third fruit when a massive spotted spider dropped out of nowhere and landed directly on the open pages of the atlas.

The thing was well over a meter long, its body covered in vivid red markings. It opened its enormous maw and lunged straight at her.

"Ahhhh!"

Lin HuanHuan lurched backward in fright. Her hands lost their grip on the branch — and she plummeted straight down.

Shuangyun, lurking not far off, shot forward with lightning speed and caught her cleanly before she hit the ground.

Lin HuanHuan, having narrowly escaped disaster, let out a shaky breath of relief.

Then she looked up and saw exactly who had caught her.

Her eyes went wide. "Why is it you again?!"

Shuangyun gave a cold snort.

Lin HuanHuan thought his attitude was absolutely asking to be punched — but he had just saved her life, so she couldn't very well say much. She settled for pushing against his chest. "Put me down."

Shuangyun noticed that the little female in his arms was soft and warm, carrying a faint, pleasant scent. The chest she'd just pressed against gave two involuntary heaves.

This was the first time he had ever held a female.

It felt considerably better than he'd imagined.

He was reluctant to let go — but he released her all the same.

The moment Lin HuanHuan's feet touched the ground, she immediately stepped back two paces, putting distance between them.

Before she could even open her mouth to say thank you, Shuangyun suddenly yanked her sideways.

The giant spider had leapt down from the tree and landed exactly where Lin HuanHuan had just been standing.

Another second and she'd have been face-to-face with it.

The moment the spider hit the ground, it launched itself at Shuangyun.

Shuangyun didn't flinch. He shifted straight into his wolf form, and his razor-sharp claws swept across the spider's abdomen in one brutal strike, tearing three long, deep gashes into its belly.

The spider let out a wretched shriek.

Fully enraged, it thrashed all eight of its long spindly legs, throwing itself at Shuangyun with everything it had.

The fight between beast and spider was ferocious.

This was the first time Lin HuanHuan had ever seen Shuangyun's beast form.

She knew he was a wolf — but she hadn't known his fur was a pure, unblemished silver-white, without a single fleck of any other colour. It shone like moonlight, impossibly beautiful.

The spider was cunning, but Shuangyun outmatched it completely. One devastating swipe sent it crashing onto its back, its round belly torn wide open, bright green viscous blood pooling across the ground.

Lin HuanHuan's stomach turned.

She averted her eyes and carefully made her way over to Shuangyun. "Thank you for saving me. I mean it."

"What good is a 'thank you'?" Shuangyun said with a derisive laugh. "I want you to repay me with action."

"Huh?"

Shuangyun leaned down toward her with a wicked grin. "How about you spread your legs and let me have a go?"

Lin HuanHuan's face went red with fury.

This guy was a complete creep!

She balled up her fists and glared at him. "You bullied me last time, and this time you saved me — we're even! We owe each other nothing!"

Shuangyun flicked his tail with complete indifference. "If we're even, then why are you still standing here? Waiting around for me to do something to you?"

"You are absolutely insufferable!"

Lin HuanHuan spun on her heel and ran.

Only after she was well and truly gone did Shuangyun slowly sink to the ground, letting his claws come into view — stained all over with spider blood.

That blood was lethally venomous. His claws had already begun turning a sinister, deep purple.

Shuangyun's dark green eyes dropped, and he let out a quiet, self-deprecating laugh.

Who would have thought — fearsome as he looked any other day — that he'd end up dying at the hands of a spider.

If the tribe ever found out, they'd never let him live it down. Well. He wouldn't be alive to see it, so perhaps that was a moot point.

He closed his eyes and prepared to meet death quietly.

Then came the sound of rapid footsteps.

Shuangyun's eyes flew open. He turned toward the sound — and saw that the little female who should have been long gone had come running back.

Something complicated surged up inside him, nameless and tangled.

But his tone was as unpleasant as ever.

"Why are you back? Don't tell me you've changed your mind and come to lie down for me after all?"

Lin HuanHuan ignored him completely.

She snatched up the atlas that had fallen to the ground. In her panic earlier, she'd left behind something important.

She tucked it away carefully and was about to turn and leave — when she noticed something was very wrong with Shuangyun.

Lin HuanHuan hesitated for a moment, then moved closer. "What's wrong with you?"

"Why are you getting so close?" Shuangyun snapped. "Don't you know I can't stand females?!"

Lin HuanHuan rolled her eyes. If this guy hadn't just saved her life, she'd have grabbed a fistful of leaves and shoved them straight into his infuriating mouth.

Though Shuangyun was doing his best to hide his claws, Lin HuanHuan's sharp eyes had already caught the discolouration.

"Are you poisoned?" she asked.

"None of your business," Shuangyun said, turning away.

Lin HuanHuan tried to get a closer look. He shoved her back with his tail.

"Stay away from me!"

Lin HuanHuan shot him one final glare — then hugged the atlas to her chest and ran.

Shuangyun watched her go without a single backward glance, clean and decisive, and a great wave of disappointment crashed over him without warning.

As expected. All females were the same — cold and unfeeling.

Even if someone gave their life for them, they wouldn't be moved.

The dark green of his eyes dimmed rapidly. His silver-white fur seemed to lose all its lustre, as though a shadow had settled over it.

His father had died for a female.

And now he was going to follow the same path.

He wasn't entirely at peace with it — but when he thought it over, it almost seemed fitting.

He chose this road. He chose to save her. Dying for it was no more than he deserved.

Shuangyun's head grew heavy and foggy, his vision blurring at the edges, his body going colder and colder.

He knew. He was about to die.

And with that thought came a despair he hadn't expected to feel.

Then Lin HuanHuan came sprinting back, arms full of herbs.

By now, Shuangyun had already lost consciousness, hovering on the edge of death.

Lin HuanHuan knelt in front of him. She drew her bone knife and made a careful incision in his paw to draw out the poisoned blood. Then she crushed the herbs she'd gathered and packed the poultice evenly over the wound.

She cut a strip of hide and bound the injury tight.
