Chapter 7 — Damn It!

Cute Beast World: Beast Husband, Memeda!

Lang Zhu smelled the scent of blood and saw Lin HuanHuan's pale face. "Quickly put her on the bed," he immediately said to Bai Di.

Bai Di placed Lin HuanHuan gently on the bed.

"Where is the wound?" Lang Zhu asked.

Just as Bai Di moved to pull aside the animal skins wrapped around Lin HuanHuan, she gripped them tightly and shrank away from his hand.

"I really didn't get hurt!" Lin HuanHuan said, her face flushed red.

"You're shedding a lot of blood," Bai Di coaxed gently. "If you don't stop the bleeding in time, you could die. Please just let the witch doctor take a look — alright?"

How could she show such a place to another person? Not even a glance!

Lin HuanHuan didn't know how to make Bai Di believe her, so she could only pin her hopes on the old witch doctor instead.

"My physique is like this," she explained, her face burning. "I bleed for a few days every month, but it doesn't affect my health at all. After a few days it stops on its own."

In her twenty years of life, this was the very first time she'd ever had to explain the characteristics of her aunt (T/N: a common Chinese euphemism for one's period) to another person.

And to two men, of all people?!

She could feel the shame radiating out of every pore as she was forced to go into more and more detail.

"Is that so," Lang Zhu said, looking genuinely fascinated. "What a remarkable thing. Is every female in your clan like this, or is it just you?"

"Everyone is like this."

"May I ask — what race are you?"

Lin HuanHuan was quiet for a long moment before squeezing out her answer.

"I'm a human being."

Things had already come this far. She really had no idea how to talk her way around it, so she might as well just tell the truth.

"A human being? I've heard of apes before, but never humans."

"Technically speaking," Lin HuanHuan said after a moment's thought, "humans and apes share the same ancestors, so we can more or less be counted as the same race."

Everyone was a primate. Close enough — they could just consider her a distant relative.

Lang Zhu didn't press further. He reached into his bag, pulled out a pouch of small red fruits, and placed it in Lin HuanHuan's hands.

"These are red berries. They nourish the blood. There's no set dosage — eat them whenever you feel weak from the blood loss."

"Thank you," Lin HuanHuan said with a bright smile.

Even bundled up tightly in animal skins, her face was fully exposed. Her fair skin, the pair of dimples at the corners of her mouth, and her clear, watery eyes made her look like something out of a painting.

A flicker of surprise crossed Lang Zhu's face.

Such a beautiful little female was truly rare. And rarer still — her personality seemed just as lovely, without a trace of the arrogance that females so often carried.

A bold idea suddenly took shape in Lang Zhu's mind.

Why not introduce Shuangyun to her?

The more he thought about it, the more he liked it. Keeping his expression carefully neutral, he turned back to Lin HuanHuan.

"Even though you say you aren't injured, it's best to be safe. I'd like you to stay here until tomorrow morning so I can check on you again."

Lin HuanHuan glanced at Bai Di, uncertain. "What do you think?"

Bai Di had no desire to linger in the wolf tribe — but this was about Lin HuanHuan's health, and that came before everything else.

He reached out and gently cupped her face. "Let's do as the witch doctor says. Don't worry — I'll be right here with you."

Lin HuanHuan popped a red berry into her mouth.

Sweet and a little sour. Delicious!

She held one out toward Bai Di's lips. "You eat one too."

"These are for you," Bai Di said, shaking his head. "You need them to replenish your blood."

"I'm not the one who's injured. You are."

Lin HuanHuan pointed to the wound on Bai Di's arm and gave him a very serious look. "You're the one who needs replenishing the most."

Bai Di had no argument for that. He relented and took a small bite — but firmly refused to eat any more.

Watching the easy affection between the two of them, Lang Zhu felt his conviction grow even stronger. A female this sweet was one in a million. If Shuangyun let her slip by, he'd regret it for the rest of his life.

In order to deal with the blood problem, Lin HuanHuan tried her hand at making herself a pair of underwear. Unfortunately, her sewing skills were so dismal that the finished product was completely unwearable.

In the end, Bai Di couldn't stand watching the struggle any longer and made her a pair himself.

The animal-skin underwear fit snugly around her hips, giving her figure soft, rounded curves.

Bai Di reached out and gave them an approving pat. "It really suits you."

Lin HuanHuan yanked her leather skirt down over them, face burning.

There were no sanitary napkins to be found anywhere, so she made do with a soft, absorbent leaf tucked inside. It wasn't exactly comfortable — but it would have to do.

…

When Shuangyun woke early the next morning, he found his mother tangled up with several males.

The ragged gasps and vulgar sounds made his stomach turn.

He didn't want to accept that the woman with the wrinkled face deliberately contorting herself into what she imagined was an alluring pose was his biological mother.

Every time he thought about the fact that he had come from her body, he wanted to claw every inch of skin off his own.

He turned and left without hesitation.

He didn't slow down until those sounds had faded far behind him. Even then, his mood remained foul.

Shuangyun wandered through the tribe with no real direction in mind.

When he finally came back to himself, he found he had already arrived at the old witch doctor's stone dwelling.

In the entire tribe, this was the one place that was always quiet.

Shuangyun pushed the door open, intending to sit inside for a while.

He hadn't taken two steps in when he spotted a young female wrapped in animal skins crouched on the floor, searching for something.

Shuangyun's expression darkened immediately. He strode forward, grabbed the female by the wrist, and hauled her upright.

"You dare come and steal from the old witch doctor's house? You've got some nerve!"

Lin HuanHuan startled badly at being wrenched off the ground without warning.

She stared at him in bewilderment. "My red berries rolled into the bushes. I was just trying to find them. I wasn't stealing anything."

The man in front of her was strikingly handsome, with short, fluffy silver hair. His deep-set brows had sharpened into a severe line with his anger.

Shuangyun didn't believe a word of it.

"Don't lie to me. You're a thief. Females like you — thinking that just because you can bear children you can do whatever you want. It's disgusting!"

Even the most docile animal had its limits. Lin HuanHuan had officially reached hers.

She glared at him, eyes blazing. "I am not a thief! Let me go, or I'll scream!"

"Then scream!" Shuangyun sneered. "Yes, one shout from a female and every male in the area will come running — but that doesn't mean you can do whatever you like! As chief of the wolf tribe, I will not allow anyone to steal within these walls. Female or not."

Lin HuanHuan's face had gone red with fury. Bai Di had been in such a rush the night before that he'd gone back to the cave to retrieve her cloth bag. At the rate things were going, he probably wouldn't be back for a while. She was on her own.

Lin HuanHuan's eyes darted to something just behind Shuangyun. "Witch doctor — are you alright?!"

Shuangyun spun around to look at the door.

In that instant, Lin HuanHuan shot upward and drove her knee straight into his chin!

Shuangyun was knocked back a step. He steadied himself, clutched his jaw, and fixed her with a furious glare.

"You — you hit me?! Despicable female!"
