Request a Pickup

Know a novel you'd love to see translated? Send it my way.

I read every request! I can't promise to pick up everything, but if a story moves me I'll do my best to bring it to life in English. Please include either a raw Chinese link or a NovelUpdates link. If a partial translation already exists, share that too so I don't duplicate work.

Powered by Formspree — free, no account needed for senders.

About This Site

Silken Thread Translations is a passion project — stories I've fallen in love with and want to share with English-speaking readers. I translate because these novels moved me and I believe they deserve to be felt beyond language.

All translations are done respectfully for fan enjoyment. If you are the original author or rights holder and have concerns, please reach out.
